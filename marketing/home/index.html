What NGOInfo does — and what it doesn't

Is this just ChatGPT with a wrapper?

No. ChatGPT is a blank box. NGOInfo is built around the NGO grant cycle — discovery, fit, drafting and donor reporting — and it knows funders' structures and expectations. In reporting especially, it works from your own documents and data and grounds figures in what you provide, rather than generating plausible-sounding text from nothing.

Will GrantPilot write my entire proposal for me?

It writes a structured first draft you then edit and finish in your own words. Think of it as a strong starting point that handles structure and donor-aware framing — not a hands-off service. The judgment, the local knowledge and the final words are yours.

What if the Fit Scan says "Not Recommended"?

Then it's done its job. The point of a Fit Scan is to save you from spending days on an opportunity you were never going to be eligible for. A clear "not worth your time" is as valuable as a green light — it frees you to focus on grants that fit.

Does the Donor Report Writer make up numbers?

No. Every figure and claim in a report traces back to the documents and indicator data you upload. If the evidence for something isn't there, it won't invent it to fill the gap — it surfaces the gap instead, so you can decide what to do.

Do I stay in control of what the funder sees?

Always. Reports move through review stages, and nothing is finalised or sent until you've read it and signed off. NGOInfo is AI-assisted, not auto-submitted — it never sends anything to a funder on your behalf.

Which funders and report formats do you support?

We list opportunities from major institutional and foundation funders, and the Donor Report Writer produces reports in funders' own formats — starting with funders such as FCDO and the National Lottery Community Fund, with more added regularly. If you need a specific funder's format, get in touch and we'll tell you where it is on our roadmap.

Who is NGOInfo for?

Small and mid-sized NGOs with limited grant-writing capacity, applying to competitive institutional and foundation funders — particularly teams that win grants and then face donor reporting without a dedicated M&E person. We work with NGOs across the UK, US, India and the wider Global South.

Do you guarantee we'll win funding?

No, and we'd be wary of anyone who does. NGOInfo helps you spend your time on the right opportunities and put your work forward clearly and in the right format. Funding decisions sit entirely with funders.
