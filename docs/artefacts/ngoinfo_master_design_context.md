1. TARGET USER

Primary Audience:

Small to mid-sized NGOs

Limited internal grant-writing capacity

Applying to institutional funders (USAID, foundations, bilateral agencies)

Based in UK, US, India, Africa, Global South

User Characteristics:

Risk-averse

Process-oriented

Documentation-heavy environment

Compliance-conscious

Skeptical of hype

Overloaded with funding portals

This is NOT:

Startup founders

Influencer creators

Casual users

B2C audience

Tone must reflect institutional credibility.

2. CORE POSITIONING

GrantPilot exists to help NGOs:

Decide whether to apply

Produce a structured proposal draft

Avoid wasting time on ineligible funding

Maintain compliance and structure

GrantPilot is NOT:

A funding guarantee

A probability engine

A donor intermediary

A “win more grants” platform

A hype AI tool

Never imply funding success.
